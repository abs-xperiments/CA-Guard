# Research Brief — CA-Guard

**Research snapshot date:** 2026-09-06

## Current market insight
AI-assisted accounting and audit are already established categories. Tally provides accounting/GST functionality and AI-assisted document-to-entry workflows; ClearTax provides reconciliation and risk-oriented workflows; Winman provides Indian CA workflow automation; Caseware is building agentic audit capabilities; MindBridge focuses on audit analytics and risk. Therefore the novelty cannot simply be “AI for accounting”.

## Research direction
CA-Guard should differentiate through a tightly scoped combination:
1. local/self-hostable processing for privacy;
2. hybrid detection rather than LLM-only judgment;
3. transparent risk fusion from independent signals;
4. evidence retrieval linked to every material finding;
5. human approval/rejection loop;
6. measurable research evaluation.

## Key India alignment
ICAI publicly publishes Standards on Auditing. SA 240 specifically discusses characteristics relevant to journal entries and adjustments, including unusual accounts, unusual users, period-end/post-closing entries, round numbers, and entries outside the normal course of business. SA 520 concerns analytical procedures, and SA 530 concerns audit sampling.

## Critical caution
These standards are reference material for an academic decision-support prototype, not permission for the software to make professional conclusions. Exact current applicability must be validated before any production claim.
