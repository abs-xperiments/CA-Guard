# Signals and the Standards on Auditing they relate to

**Status:** guidance, written 2026-10-07. It is not a compliance mapping and not professional advice. It should be reviewed by a practising CA before anyone relies on it externally.

The explanation card shows "Relates to SA …" under a signal only when a public ICAI Standard on Auditing names the same characteristic. If no standard names it, the card claims none. No paragraph numbers are cited, because they were not verified against the current ICAI text.

| Signal | Card wording | Relates to | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| `missing_evidence` | No supporting document | SA 500 (audit evidence); SA 240 (entries with little or no explanation) | SA 500 concerns sufficient appropriate evidence. SA 240's list of journal-entry characteristics includes entries with little or no explanation or description. |
| `period_end_concentration` | Year-end adjustment | SA 240 | Entries made at the end of a reporting period |
| `post_close_entry` | Posted after close | SA 240 | Post-closing entries |
| `rare_account_pair` | Unusual account pairing | SA 240 | Entries to unrelated, unusual or seldom-used accounts |
| `round_amount` | Round amount | SA 240 | Entries containing round numbers or consistent ending numbers |
| `unusual_preparer_account` | Outside preparer's area | SA 240 | Entries made by individuals who do not usually make journal entries |
| `amount_outlier` | Unusual amount for the account | SA 520 | Analytical procedures: an amount that differs from the expected pattern |
| `duplicate_entry`, `off_hours_posting`, `weekend_posting`, `threshold_adjacent`, `ml_anomaly` | — | none claimed | These are firm-level review heuristics, not characteristics a standard names |
